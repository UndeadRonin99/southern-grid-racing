04 / BEFORE THE GREEN LIGHT

A FEW THINGS
WORTH KNOWING.

New here? You’re in good company.

Do I need to be fast to join?

No. New and experienced drivers are welcome. Our championships will use multiple splits so drivers can compete with others of similar pace. Clean, respectful driving matters at every level.

Is the first season really free?

Yes. Season 01 will be free to enter. Our focus is building a strong racing community. You will need your own copy of Le Mans Ultimate and any content required by the confirmed calendar.

When does Season 01 start?

The calendar and start date have not been announced. Join Discord for confirmed dates, circuits, car classes, race lengths and registration details. Event times will be published in SAST (UTC+2).

How do I register?

Registration is not open yet. Join the Discord server, read the rules, and follow the registration channel. The entry link and process will be shared there once confirmed.

Can I join without a team?

You can join the community now, with or without a team. Use our find-a-team channel to meet other drivers. The championship’s team format and entry requirements will be announced before registration opens.

Is this only for South African drivers?

Southern Grid Racing is rooted in South Africa, with race times published in SAST. Drivers who share our commitment to clean racing are welcome in the community; event requirements will be listed with the championship details.
